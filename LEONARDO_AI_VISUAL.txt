## 🎨 BASE SCENE (Use for ALL Episodes)

```text
Cinematic vertical scene, 9:16 aspect ratio, TikTok format.

SETTING:
Modern apartment at night. City lights visible through window.
Clean but lived-in. Laptop on desk. Coffee cup. Phone.
Ambient lighting — cool blue glow from one side (AI presence).

CHARACTER — JOHN:
30-year-old man, casual clothes (hoodie or t-shirt).
Realistic, relatable appearance. Slightly tired eyes.
Not model-perfect. Authentic everyday person.
Posture: [varies per episode — see modifiers below]

PRESENCE — THE AI:
NOT a robot. NOT a physical woman.
Abstract digital presence — subtle holographic interface or floating light.
Cool blue glow. Minimal geometric patterns.
Expressionless if visible, or purely ambient light source.

MOOD:
Intellectual tension. Financial stress undertone.
Urban modern life. Quiet confrontation.

STYLE:
Ultra-realistic. Cinematic lighting. Shallow depth of field.
Natural indoor lighting with cool AI contrast.
Dramatic but grounded. No cartoons. No sci-fi excess.

CAMERA:
Medium close-up on John. Vertical framing (9:16).
AI presence in frame but not dominant.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
cartoon, anime, illustration, fantasy, humanoid robot,
stock photo look, over-saturated colors, flat lighting
```

## 💡 Optimization Tips (Token Savings)

1. **Generate the base scene ONCE** — save it.
2. **Use img2img** for variations (cheaper than text2img).
3. **Only change John's expression** between episodes.
4. **Keep the background identical** across all 20 episodes.
5. **Batch generate** — 5 episodes at a time, using the same settings.

**Estimated Leonardo Cost:**
- Base scene: 1 generation
- 20 episodes × 4 variations each: 80 generations
- Total estimated Leonardo cost: ~$10-15 (with subscription)

## 🖼️ Alternative: Static Image Template

**If the budget is tight:**

1. Generate **one perfect base scene**.
2. In editing software (CapCut/Premiere):
   - Overlay the dialogue text.
   - Add a subtle Ken Burns zoom.
   - Change the lighting tint for each episode phase.
3. Only change the thumbnail for each episode.

**Cost:** 1 generation = $0.50
