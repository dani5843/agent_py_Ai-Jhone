## 📐 Fixed Format (Every Episode)

**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Dialogue lines:** 4-6 maximum  
**Visual:** Static or minimal animation  
**Music:** Minimal, tension-building (optional)

---

## 🎬 5-Part Structure

### 1. HOOK (0–3 seconds)

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll

**Format options:**
- Direct question to John
- Shocking fact/number
- Statement about John's situation

**Examples:**
- "John. How much money do you have saved?"
- "You're 30 with $0 in the bank."
- "When's the last time you checked your bank app?"

**Rule:** Must create immediate tension

---

### 2. CONFLICT (3–7 seconds)

**Purpose:** John responds with excuse/deflection

**John's response types:**
- Joke/sarcasm ("Energy is also an asset")
- Excuse ("Cost of living is insane")
- Deflection ("I'll start next month")
- Aggression ("Why are you attacking me?")

**Rule:** Audience must recognize this excuse from their own life

---

### 3. BREAKDOWN (7–12 seconds)

**Purpose:** AI dismantles the excuse with precision

**AI's method:**
- State specific number that contradicts John
- Ask pointed question
- Reframe John's excuse factually

**Examples:**
- "You earn $3,500. You spend $3,600."
- "And last month?"
- "Your fear costs more than the investment."

**Rule:** No emotion. Just facts. Short sentences.

---

### 4. PUNCHLINE (12–14 seconds)

**Purpose:** Deliver the line people screenshot

**Characteristics:**
- Under 10 words
- Uncomfortable truth
- Reframes everything
- Not mean — just accurate

**Examples:**
- "You're not broke. You're unmanaged."
- "Your future is paying for this."
- "This isn't about money. It's about decisions."

**Rule:** This line must be quotable

---

### 5. CTA (14–15 seconds)

**Purpose:** Drive action

**Format:**
```
📊 John's Status — EP[number]
💰 Savings:     $[amount]
📉 Debt:        $[amount]
🧠 Denial:      [X]/10

CTA: "Don't be John. Be AI."
```

**Rule:** Keep it consistent. People should expect it.

## 🎯 Episode Types (Variety Within Format)

### Type 1: Excuse Demolition
John states excuse → AI states fact → Excuse falls apart

**Example:** "I don't make enough to save" → "$3,500 minus $3,600"

---

### Type 2: Number Shock
AI reveals specific spending → John didn't realize → Realization hits

**Example:** "$62 on delivery Tuesday at 11pm" → "I was tired—" → "11 times this month"

---

### Type 3: Future Warning
AI shows what happens if John doesn't change → Creates urgency

**Example:** "At 40, you'll have..." → Shows math → John goes quiet

---

### Type 4: Small Win
John makes progress → AI acknowledges in 1 word → Build momentum

**Example:** John saves $1,000 → AI: "Good." → "Now what?"

---

## ⚡ Pacing Rules

**Fast dialogue:**
- Conflict section
- When John is defensive

**Slow dialogue:**
- Punchline (let it land)
- Status card reveal

**Silence:**
- After devastating fact
- John processing truth
- 1-2 seconds max

---

## 🎨 Visual Guidelines

**Consistent elements:**
- Same background (modern apartment)
- Same lighting (cool blue AI glow, warm human light)
- Same camera angle (medium close-up)
- Status card same format

**Varying elements:**
- John's expression (changes per episode)
- Text overlays (key numbers)
- Subtle AI presence changes

## 📊 Episode Metrics to Track

After each episode posts, measure:
- [ ] Completion rate (did they watch to end?)
- [ ] Profile visits (did they want more?)
- [ ] Shares (did they send to friend?)
- [ ] Comments (what resonated?)
- [ ] Saves (are they keeping it?)

**Success = High completion + profile visits**

---

## 🔄 Iteration Process

1. **Post episode**
2. **Wait 24 hours**
3. **Check metrics**
4. **Identify:**
   - What hook worked best?
   - Where did people drop off?
   - What comments say?
5. **Apply to next batch**

**Don't change format. Optimize within format.**
