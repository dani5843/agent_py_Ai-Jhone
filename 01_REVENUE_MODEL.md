## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

**Risk 1: Platform dependency**
- **Solution:** Cross-post to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Solution:** Build email list from digital product buyers

**Risk 2: Affiliate link changes**
- **Solution:** Use link management (Linktree, Stan)
- **Solution:** Diversify affiliate partners

**Risk 3: Sponsorship rejection**
- **Solution:** Don't rely on sponsors until Stream 1+2 work
- **Solution:** Focus on audience first, sponsors follow
