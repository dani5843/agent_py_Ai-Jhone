## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

**Risk 1: Platform Dependency**

- **Solution:** Cross-post to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- **Solution:** Build an email list from digital product buyers

**Risk 2: Affiliate Link Changes**

- **Solution:** Use link management tools (Linktree, Stan)
- **Solution:** Diversify affiliate partners

**Risk 3: Sponsorship Rejection**

- **Solution:** Don't rely on sponsors until Streams 1 and 2 are generating revenue
- **Solution:** Focus on building the audience first; sponsors will follow
