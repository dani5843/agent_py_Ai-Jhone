# TONE GUIDE

## 🎭 Overall Series Tone

**"A conversation no one wants to have but everyone needs."**

Not educational. Not preachy. Not motivational.  
**Cynical, intelligent, authentic.**

---

## ⚡ Dialogue Rules

### THE AI

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Keep it short** | Max 2 sentences per response |
| **No lectures** | If explaining needed → state numbers, not essays |
| **No encouragement** | She doesn't advise. She states facts. |
| **Sarcasm — minimal** | One sharp line. Never a barrage. |
| **Every scene = punchline** | Dialogue must end with something that stings |

---

### JOHN

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Always has excuse** | Must be ready in advance |
| **Not stupid** | Defensive, not ridiculous |
| **Slight drama** | Exaggerated slightly — but not cartoon |
| **Vulnerability moments** | Once per episode, one line revealing real fear |

---

## 🚫 What to Avoid

❌ **AI saying "I understand"** → No. She doesn't.  
❌ **Long explanations** → Number + sentence. Done.  
❌ **John understanding too fast** → His resistance IS the series.  
❌ **Preachy messages** → Audience will leave.  
❌ **Humor that insults** → We laugh WITH John. Not AT John.

---

## ✅ Right Tone vs Wrong Tone

### ❌ WRONG

> **AI:** "John, I understand this is hard. But if you just start saving 10% of your salary, in one year you'll have an amazing emergency fund!"

**Why wrong:**
- Too encouraging
- Too long
- Sounds like a self-help book

---

### ✅ RIGHT

> **AI:** "You spend $300 more than you earn. Every month."  
> **John:** "That only happened this month—"  
> **AI:** "And last month."

**Why right:**
- Short
- Specific number
- Catches him in lie
- No emotion

## 🎬 Episode Structure (15 seconds)

**[מבנה קבוע לכל פרק - TikTok format]**

```
0–3 sec   →  HOOK — question/fact that hits hard
3–7 sec   →  CONFLICT — John defends/makes excuse
7–12 sec  →  BREAKDOWN — AI dismantles excuse with facts
12–14 sec →  PUNCHLINE — line people will screenshot
14–15 sec →  CTA — "Don't be John. Be AI."
```

---

## 💡 What Makes a Hook Viral

**A hook = question the viewer lives with but never asked out loud**

### Examples:

✅ "You're 30 with $0 saved. What exactly is the plan?"  
✅ "How many times did you decide to start saving this month?"  
✅ "When's the last time you opened your bank app?"  
✅ "You know how much waiting one year costs?"  
✅ "The money you spent on coffee in 3 years — want to hear?"

---

## 🎯 Punchline Formula

**Best punchlines are:**
1. **Short** (under 10 words)
2. **Specific** (use numbers when possible)
3. **Uncomfortably true** (audience thinks "ouch")
4. **Not insulting** (observation, not attack)

### Examples:

> "You're not living above your means. You're living without means."

> "Your fear is working against you."

> "This isn't about money. It's about decisions."

> "You're paying for a feeling of life you're not actually living."

---

## 📝 Dialogue Testing Checklist

Before finalizing any script, ask:

- [ ] Does the AI sound cold and precise?
- [ ] Does John sound relatable (not dumb)?
- [ ] Is there a specific number in the dialogue?
- [ ] Does it end with a punchline?
- [ ] Would someone screenshot this?
- [ ] Is it under 15 seconds when read aloud?

## 🎤 Voice Direction (להקלטות)

### AI Voice:
- **Tone:** Flat, even, no inflection at end of sentences
- **Pace:** Moderate, consistent
- **Emotion:** Zero
- **Think:** Siri, but sharper

### John Voice:
- **Tone:** Natural, conversational
- **Pace:** Varies — faster when defensive, slower when caught
- **Emotion:** Real (not overacted)
- **Think:** Your friend making excuses

---

## 🌍 Language Notes (Global English Audience)

**Avoid:**
- Heavy American slang
- Regional idioms
- Complex financial jargon

**Use:**
- Simple, clear English
- Universal money concepts
- Relatable situations globally

**Examples:**
✅ "delivery apps" not "DoorDash"  
✅ "investing" not "401k" (unless explaining)  
✅ "broke" not "skint" (too British)
